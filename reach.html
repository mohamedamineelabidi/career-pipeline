Logged-in LinkedIn scan

The LinkedIn scan with your own session runs only from the terminal, with a hard limit of 10 profiles per target. Nothing on this page opens LinkedIn on your behalf.

uv run python reach/linkedin_people_scan.py --target <name> --limit 10

Safety note: the scan reads public profile text visible to your account and stores it locally. It never sends connection requests or messages.
