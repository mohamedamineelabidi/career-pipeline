      Guide

      What this platform is

      Career Pipeline is a local, private job-search command center for one person. It runs only on this computer (127.0.0.1:8786), stores everything in one SQLite file, and never sends anything on your behalf.

      • It finds jobs from RSS feeds, JobSpy-style boards, and pasted listings, then fetches the full description.
      • It scores them against your real evidence profile (semantic match, skill gaps, optional Groq rubric score).
      • It prepares your material: one-page ATS CV, cover-letter drafts, interview prep, contact outreach drafts, pre-filled application forms.
      • You do the final step yourself: open the listing, review, and click Apply or Send on the real website. The app then tracks it once you mark "applied by me".

      How a job moves through the pipeline

      Every opportunity has one status. Statuses only move through allowed transitions, and each move is recorded in the activity timeline.

      Discovered Verified Eligible Shortlisted Applied by you Closed
      StatusMeaningWhat you do next
      DiscoveredFound by a feed, a board, or pasted by you. Not checked yet.Open the listing (click the title). If it is real and still open, move to Verified.
      VerifiedThe posting exists and is active.Read the requirements. If you can honestly apply, move to Eligible; otherwise Close it.
      EligibleYou match the hard requirements.Generate the CV in the drawer, check the gaps, then Shortlist the ones worth your time.
      ShortlistedYour target list.Prepare the form (Apply prep tab), open the listing, apply yourself, then mark "applied by me" in the Activity tab.
      Applied by youYou confirmed you submitted it.Follow up after 7 days if silent (the Tracker flags it). Log the outcome.
      ClosedRejected, expired, or not for you.Nothing. It stays in the data for the Insights statistics.

      The details drawer

      Opens from the company line, the Details button, or a Tracker card. Tabs: Description (full JD), Score breakdown, Keywords, CV (generate, preview, open PDF, next steps), Interview prep, Cover letter, Apply prep (pre-filled form data, never submitted), Contacts, Activity (timeline and "Mark as applied by me"). The header has an Open job listing link.

      Recommended daily routine (15 to 25 minutes)

      1. Read the nightly digest.The pipeline runs every day at 08:30 and prints: new jobs, verified, scored, drafts due today. On the Overview page the Pipeline run card shows the same. Press Run now if it says Never run.
      2. Triage Discovered.Tracker, Discovered column, sorted by fit. Open each title (new tab). Real and open: Update status to Verified. Junk, expired, wrong country: Closed. Aim for 10 to 20 decisions.
      3. Qualify Verified.Read the JD in the drawer. Look at Score breakdown and Gaps. If you honestly meet the must-haves: Eligible. If not: Closed. Do not stretch the truth.
      4. Prepare Eligible.CV tab: Generate, then Preview. If gaps are only nice-to-haves, Shortlist it. Optionally Cover letter and Interview prep.
      5. Apply yourself.Apply prep tab gives the field values. Open the listing, fill the real form, attach the PDF from the CVs page, submit on their site. Back here: Activity tab, Mark as applied by me.
      6. Follow up.Tracker filter Follow up shows applications silent for 7+ days. Drafts page has a follow-up text. Send it yourself, then mark the step sent.
      7. Weekly: Insights.Check conversion, source quality, repost patterns, and the skill gaps list. Adjust where you spend time.

      Scores explained

      ScoreHow it is computedHow to read it
      Semantic fit (0-100)Local MiniLM embeddings compare the job description with your evidence profile, plus skills taxonomy matching. Runs offline.60+ good, 40-59 borderline, below 40 usually a poor match or a JD that was not fetched.
      AI fit (0-100)Groq model (openai/gpt-oss-120b) scores the JD against your profile with a fixed rubric: must-haves, nice-to-haves, seniority, location. Only runs when a GROQ_API_KEY is set. The JD text is sent to Groq.Shown as "AI 60" on cards. More nuanced than semantic fit, but slower and rate-limited (about 3 calls per minute).
      Priority (0-100)Heuristic mix of fit, freshness, source confidence, and deadline.Used for default sorting. Not a truth about the job.
      GapsSkills the JD requests that are absent from your evidence.Read them before applying. They never get added to the CV automatically.
      Scores to re-check (Insights)Jobs where semantic fit and AI fit differ by 25 or more.One of the two is probably wrong. Read the JD yourself.

      Safety rules the app enforces

      Never applies, sends, or connectsThere is no Send, Apply, Submit, or Connect button anywhere. A test fails the build if one appears.
      Drafts stay localOutreach, cover letters, and follow-ups are text you copy. Steps are marked "sent by you" only after you confirm.
      Truthful CVOne page, one column, built only from your evidence profile. No invented tools, metrics, or titles.
      Respects websitesNo login-wall bypass, no CAPTCHA solving, polite delays. LinkedIn and Glassdoor descriptions are skipped on purpose.
      Localhost onlyThe server binds to 127.0.0.1 and rejects cross-origin requests. Your data never leaves the machine except the JD text sent to Groq when AI scoring is on.
      No lost updatesEvery change carries a version. If the row changed underneath you, the app says "Refreshed, try again" instead of overwriting.

      Under the hood

      • Backend: Python 3.11 standard library HTTP server (pipeline_v2.py), SQLite database career_pipeline_v2.sqlite3 (schema v11), FTS search.
      • Frontend: this single HTML file, vanilla JavaScript, system fonts, no CDN, works offline. All text is rendered as plain text nodes; the page never builds DOM from HTML strings.
      • Discovery: rss_sources.py (Jobicy, RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely, company feeds), JobSpy-style board readers, Agent Reach reader, paste import.
      • Descriptions: fetch_job_descriptions.py with Jina reader fallback and a deny-list for login-walled sites.
      • Matching: semantic_match.py (MiniLM embeddings), skills_taxonomy.json, keyword_highlight.
      • AI: llm_client.py shared Groq client, llm_scoring.py rubric scorer, recruiter review and CV improvement loop.
      • CV: RenderCV-style one-page ATS template, PDF via the renderer, preview PNG via pypdfium2.
      • Runner: pipeline_runner.py, staged and resumable: discover, fetch, match, score, digest. Exposed at /api/pipeline/run and /latest. Nightly at 08:30 through a zero-token scheduled job.
      • Analytics: analytics.py, six read-only functions behind /api/analytics/summary (60 s cache).
      • Tests: uv run python -m pytest tests -q. UI checks with real Playwright scripts in scripts/ (ui_check_cvs.py, ui_check_drawer_cv.py, ui_check_sidebar.py, ui_check_title_links.py, ui_measure.py).

      Known limits, stated plainly

      • LinkedIn job descriptions are behind a login wall and are not fetched. Those jobs show Fit 0 until you paste the description yourself.
      • Groq scoring is rate-limited (about 3 calls per minute). A run of 40 jobs takes around 15 minutes. One in forty calls may fail on malformed JSON and is retried on the next run.
      • The eligibility label is a heuristic, not legal advice about visas or contracts.
      • Contacts come only from public, verifiable sources; the app never guesses email addresses.
      • The dashboard is designed for a laptop screen (1280 px and above). Below 1024 px the sidebar becomes an overlay.
